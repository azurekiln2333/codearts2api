# Technical proposal

Compare the old and repaired request paths against live fingerprints. Propose a safe build/config diagnostic and runbook that identifies the checked-out commit and running image, preserves auths/data/config/.env, then rebuilds/recreates only the intended service when separately authorized.

Extend actual-handler saturation coverage with 18 mixed-model requests and partially cooling accounts. Reproduce a current-code defect before editing. Preserve explicit config/env precedence and existing lifecycle resource ownership. Reducing concurrency cannot substitute for proving cleanup.

The confirmed MaxRotate gap is scoped to capability failures: do not charge an account/model-specific rejection against the generic provider-failure rotation cap. The finite tried-account set and startup deadline still bound the operation. Keep admission capacity counting enabled accounts (including cooling ones), as required by the existing lifecycle spec.

Silent-upstream bounds do not impose a total generation deadline: after preflight an active upstream can legitimately keep a non-streaming aggregation open past startup. Do not infer old retry cadence from completion times alone. Deployed-path fingerprints are stronger evidence than this timing speculation.
